Registration Process

Sign up and schedule your first donation with ease

Step 1: Create an Account

Sign up with your basic details such as name, email, phone number, and password.

Step 2: Fill Personal Details

Enter your age, gender, blood group, and location to verify eligibility.

Step 3: Medical Questionnaire

Complete a quick health and lifestyle questionnaire to assess your suitability for donation.

Step 4: Schedule Your Donation

Choose a nearby blood donation center and schedule a convenient time slot.

Step 5: Confirmation

You will receive a confirmation message and instructions for the day of donation.
