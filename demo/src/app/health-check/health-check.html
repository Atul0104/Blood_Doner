Health Scanning Before Blood Donation

A simple check-up to ensure you're ready to donate safely

1. Medical History Review

You’ll be asked about your medical background, medications, and recent travel to assess donation eligibility.

2. Physical Examination

A basic health screening includes checking blood pressure, pulse, temperature, and weight.

3. Hemoglobin Level Test

A quick blood test from your fingertip is used to measure your hemoglobin levels to ensure you can safely donate.

4. Final Eligibility Check

After all checks, the medical team will determine if you're fit for donation that day.
