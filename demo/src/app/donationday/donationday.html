Donation Day

Relax as our professional staff guide you through the process

Arrival & Check-In

When you arrive at the donation center, our staff will greet you and verify your identity and appointment details.

Pre-Donation Review

A final health screening will be conducted, including checking your blood pressure, pulse, and hemoglobin levels.

The Donation Process

The actual donation takes about 10 minutes. You'll be comfortably seated while a professional collects your blood.

Post-Donation Refreshments

After donating, enjoy some snacks and rest for 10-15 minutes to ensure you’re feeling well before leaving.
